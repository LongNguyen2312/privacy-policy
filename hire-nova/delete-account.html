HireNova - Account Deletion

Users can delete their account directly from the HireNova application.

When an account deletion request is submitted:

  • Your account will be deactivated immediately.
  • You will no longer be able to sign in or access your account.
  • Your interview history and uploaded CVs will be permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Certain billing or transaction records may be retained if required by applicable laws or regulations.

If you have an active subscription, deleting your account does not automatically cancel your Google Play or App Store subscription. Please cancel your subscription separately through your respective store account.
