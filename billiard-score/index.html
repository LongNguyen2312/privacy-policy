Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

CueBoard is a billiard scorekeeping application that helps users keep track of billiard matches, including Pool (Race to) and Cards/Lá scoring.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use CueBoard.

1. Information We Collect

CueBoard does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

The App may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Game scores and match results
  • Player names entered by the user
  • Match dates and game details
  • App language and theme preferences
  • In-progress Cards/Lá match data

2. How We Use Your Information

Information stored by CueBoard is used solely to provide the App's functionality, including:

  • Keeping track of scores during a match
  • Saving match history
  • Displaying previous match details
  • Resuming an in-progress match
  • Saving language and appearance preferences

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

3. Data Storage

CueBoard stores app data locally on your device using local device storage.

We do not operate a server that receives or stores your match history or player information.

If you uninstall the App or clear its app data, locally stored information may be permanently deleted.

4. Photos and Videos

CueBoard does not require access to your device's photo or video library for its core functionality.

The App does not collect, upload, or store your personal photos or videos.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Because CueBoard does not transmit your locally stored match data to our servers, your scores and player information remain on your device.

6. Third-Party Services

CueBoard does not currently use third-party services that collect personal information from users.

If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant services and data practices.

7. Children's Privacy

CueBoard does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Because the App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by the App. Since match data and preferences are stored locally on your device, the security of that data also depends on the security of your device and operating system.

9. Your Choices

You can delete locally stored match history through the App's history settings, where available.

You can also remove all locally stored App data by uninstalling CueBoard or clearing the App's data through your device settings.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to CueBoard or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be made available through the privacy policy URL associated with the App.
